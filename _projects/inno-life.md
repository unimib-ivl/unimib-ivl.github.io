INNO-LIFE is a research and development project on innovative nutrition and lifestyle strategies for disease prevention and human wellness, funded by Regione Lombardia under the "Collabora & Innova" call.

The project is led by ATON IT S.r.l., with Planeat S.r.l. SB, Crescendo Care S.r.l., ICS Maugeri S.p.A. SB, Neosperience Health S.p.A., Solongevity Healthcenters S.r.l. and the University of Milano-Bicocca.

Our involvement concerns food computing: the analysis of food images and data with computer vision and artificial intelligence.
