bio: |-
  Gianluigi Ciocca is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at the University of Milano-Bicocca. In 2023, he obtained the Italian National Scientific Qualification (Abilitazione Scientifica Nazionale) for the position of Full Professor in the academic recruitment fields 01/B1 and 09/H1. After graduating in Information Science, he conducted research at the National Research Council (CNR) in Milan, focusing on the analysis, indexing, and retrieval of images and multimedia content. He subsequently continued his academic career at the University of Milano-Bicocca, where he held a position as Assistant Professor from 2007 and has been an Associate Professor since 2015.

  His research interests include computer vision, machine learning, and multimedia, with particular emphasis on image understanding, image quality assessment, food image analysis, computer graphics, 3D reconstruction, and remote sensing. He has authored 159 publications, including 52 journal articles, 30 of which have been published in Q1 journals. His Google Scholar h-index is 34, with over 5,200 citations. He has held scientific leadership roles in national research projects and industrial collaborations and is a co-inventor of three patents. He has also undertaken various scientific coordination and supervisory responsibilities for research fellows, research associates, and postdoctoral researchers. He serves as Associate Editor-in-Chief for the Computer Vision area of Neurocomputing (a Q1 journal in Computer Science). Since 2021, he has served as Area Chair of the International Conference on Computer Vision Theory and Applications (B-ranked conference), and in 2022 he served as Program Chair of the British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC, A-ranked conference).
