bio: >-
  Claudio Rota is a postdoctoral fellow at the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication (DISCo) of the University of Milano-Bicocca (Italy). His main research interests concern image and video enhancement and restoration. He obtained his Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Milano-Bicocca in 2025, focusing on studying deep learning methods for video restoration. In 2023, he spent six months as a visiting student at the Computer Vision Center (CVC) in Barcelona (Spain). He obtained his Bachelor’s and Master’s Degrees in Computer Science at the University of Milano-Bicocca in 2019 and 2021, respectively. In the first hear as a postdoctoral fellow, he worked on the [Statistical Machine Learning for Exposure Development (SMILE)](https://smile.mi.imati.cnr.it/) project, focusing on building segmentation from aerial imagery. He is currently working on visual scene analysis and rendering, with an emphasis on illumination modeling and relighting.
