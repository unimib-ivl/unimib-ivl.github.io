Imaging and Vision Laboratory
University of Milano-Bicocca

Understanding the world through signals

Founded in 2003 within the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication at the University of Milano-Bicocca, the Imaging and Vision Laboratory (IVL) develops methods and systems for capturing, enhancing, analyzing, and organizing visual information.

Our research

Our work spans color and multispectral imaging, image quality and enhancement, computer and machine vision, and visual information management. We pursue fundamental research alongside practical applications and maintain long-standing collaborations with international industry partners.
