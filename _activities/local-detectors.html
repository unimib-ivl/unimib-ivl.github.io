Local Visual detectors and descriptors have been studied for many years, but their applications, for example on mobile visual search, for large volume, low cost, low power embedded systems have been limited or negligible to date. One reason is the lack of an industry standard applicable at worldwide level. MPEG Compact Descriptors for Visual Search (CDVS) working group is filling this gap by defining a highly performing extraction stage and the bitstream syntax at its output in order to achieve interoperability between different implementation of clients and servers. As a contribution to such process, in our previous work we presented a review of existing methods for gray-levels interest point detection and description. This article extends our previous analysis in the framework of the MPEG CDVS Test Model considering further detectors and the use of color descriptors.
