Color constancy algorithms are typically evaluated with a statistical analysis of the recovery angular error and the reproduction angular error between the estimated and ground truth illuminants. Such analysis provides information about only the magnitude of the errors, and not about their chromatic properties. We propose an Angle-Retaining Chromaticity diagram (ARC) for the visual analysis of the estimated illuminants and the corresponding errors.

We provide an official MATLAB implementation, which can be used to convert RGB data into our ARC representation, and viceversa:
