AugLy includes various specific transformations that users perform in real life on Internet platforms such as Facebook, for example when they turn an image into a meme, overlay text/emoji on images/videos, or repost a screenshot from social media. Although AugLy also contains more generic transformations, it is particularly useful for working on issues such as copy detection, hate speech detection, or copyright infringement.
