Paper submission




The Workshop Proceedings will be published in the Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) series, indexed as peer-reviewed publication in the Web of Science.

Please, note that we now welcome papers in the 12-15 pages range for the proceedings.

Each accepted Workshop paper requires that at least one author is registered to the workshop.


The final manuscripts should be prepared using LaTeX2e and the author kit provided by Springer.

  • LaTeX2e paper model: [download]
  • LNCS proceedings guidelines: [download]

It is possible, but discouraged, to use other systems for the preparation of papers, e.g. MS-Word. Author kits may be found on the Springer LNCS site, under "Information for Authors of Springer Computer Science Proceedings".
